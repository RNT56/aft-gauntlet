# Research and Asset Sources

Use this registry as a search seed. Verify current pages and select only project-relevant deep links.

## Finished-Product References

Find real competitors and products through direct search, maps/local results, App Store/Google Play listings, game storefronts, platform showcases, portfolios, and official product sites. Open the actual product or flow whenever possible. Do not use awards galleries as the sole bar.

For each selected reference, state the judging dimension, such as market credibility, offer clarity, onboarding, booking, checkout, navigation, visual composition, motion, game feel, native behavior, or failure recovery.

## Asset Decision Record

For each asset or component carried into a generated prompt, capture:

- Exact source URL
- Intended role
- Why it fits the bar and brand
- License/access status
- Required adaptation
- Responsive/accessibility fallback
- Performance implications
- Whether the final builder may replace it with a demonstrably stronger option

Require `SOURCE_NOTES.md` or an equivalent ledger in the final build.
