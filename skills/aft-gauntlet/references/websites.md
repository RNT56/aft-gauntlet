# Website Platform Pack

## Research

Select references in three layers:

- Direct competitors for market language, offers, trust, and expected flows
- Best-in-class finished sites for art direction, storytelling, conversion, and responsive craft
- Feature products for booking, checkout, configuration, maps, search, messaging, or other required interactions

Inspect full pages and real flows at desktop and mobile sizes. Component demos are ingredients, not finished-site quality bars.

## Business Truth

Make the prompt establish audience, customer jobs, objections, content language, primary conversion, offers, pricing logic, proof, operational constraints, and authorization boundaries. For local businesses, research local search intent, competitors, directions, hours, phone/messaging behavior, booking expectations, FAQs, and appropriate structured data.

Never fabricate reviews, awards, certifications, staff, addresses, availability, legal text, ratings, or performance claims. Centralize concept data and identify owner-confirmation items.

## Brand Invention Authority

Do not infer permission to invent a brand, name, or business merely because the brief omits one. If and only if the original user input explicitly requests brand invention, make the prompt authorize original naming, positioning, narrative, voice, and visual identity based on the supplied context and current research. When material, generate distinct candidates and use fresh critics to compare specificity, memorability, audience fit, extensibility, and visual potential.

Treat the selected identity as a creative concept until the owner confirms it. Keep all real-world claims and operational details separate, and never claim trademark, domain, social-handle, or legal availability without authorized current checks.

## Visual Direction

Require a visual north star before implementation when no established design system exists. Judge whole-page composition, not isolated cards.

Treat spatial and background composition as authored:

- Reject interchangeable stacks of rectangular sections unless deliberately supported by the art direction.
- Derive shape, rhythm, texture, depth, and motion from the brand, place, subject, materials, or product.
- Consider responsive SVG, masks, clip paths, layered media, foreground crossings, environmental silhouettes, restrained texture, and light.
- Reject generic waves, random blobs, repetitive gradients, default library backgrounds, or effects transferable unchanged to unrelated brands.
- Preserve readability, performance, mobile composition, and reduced motion.

## Ingredient Selection

Use relevant sources from `research-sources.md`. Require a scout to map each candidate to a customer or storytelling purpose. Normalize selected components to one design system. Do not mandate a count or install multiple motion libraries for the same job.

## Real Artifact

Critics inspect:

- Running production build
- Full-page desktop and mobile screenshots
- Hover, touch, keyboard, focus, dialogs, menus, and forms
- Loading, empty, error, offline, and unavailable states
- Long and short content
- Reduced motion and data-saving behavior
- SEO output, structured data, accessibility, and performance profiles

## Typical Gates

Select only relevant gates and make them measurable:

- Comprehension and information scent
- Brand specificity and visual coherence
- Conversion path completion
- Responsive composition
- WCAG 2.2 AA behavior
- LCP, CLS, INP, asset weight, and offscreen work
- Form validation and honest success/failure behavior
- Search metadata and schema validity
- Consent and privacy behavior
- Asset licenses and attribution
- Production build and deployment readiness

For commerce, add discovery, configuration, price, cart, checkout, payment, confirmation, cancellation, refund, inventory, and support flows. For SaaS, add onboarding, account states, core task completion, permissions, billing, and failure recovery.
