# Games Platform Pack

## Quality Bars

Use shipped, playable games and direct gameplay footage—not screenshots alone. Select separate references for the game's genre, camera, control scheme, art direction, interaction model, and target platform. Compare only dimensions that genuinely transfer; do not clone protected art, levels, characters, audio, or trade dress.

## Lead-Agent Freedom

State the intended player experience, target platforms, genre, camera, audience, session length, input methods, content boundaries, and performance constraints. Let the lead agent choose engine, architecture, systems, and decomposition unless the user fixes them.

## Independent Gauntlets

The lead should discover the workstreams, typically including some of:

- Core loop and game feel
- Movement, camera, input, and controls
- Combat or primary interaction
- Physics and collision
- Enemies, NPCs, and AI
- Level design, pacing, navigation, and encounters
- Art direction, environments, characters, animation, VFX, lighting, and materials
- UI, HUD, menus, onboarding, accessibility, and settings
- Audio, music, spatial sound, and feedback
- Save/load, progression, economy, multiplayer, or backend when relevant
- Optimization, packaging, crash recovery, and target-device support

Do not prescribe these as a fixed roster. Give each important system its own builder, fresh playtest critic, and integration pass.

## Real Artifact

Critics must play the build or inspect direct gameplay capture plus input/performance evidence. Judge:

- Responsiveness and input latency
- Movement and camera feel
- Readability and feedback
- Challenge, pacing, balance, and fun
- Animation timing and transitions
- Visual hierarchy, lighting, VFX, and environment coherence
- Audio response and mix
- Bugs, exploits, soft locks, and recovery
- Frame time, memory, load time, shader stutter, and stability
- Controller, keyboard/mouse, touch, or accessibility behavior as applicable

Use blind playtest comparisons when possible. A critic should not see the builder's rationale.

## Asset Research

Research exact relevant assets and licenses from sources such as:

- Fab: https://www.fab.com/
- Unity Asset Store: https://assetstore.unity.com/
- Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/
- Mixamo: https://www.mixamo.com/
- Kenney: https://kenney.nl/assets
- Quaternius: https://quaternius.com/
- Freesound: https://freesound.org/
- OpenGameArt: https://opengameart.org/
- itch.io game assets: https://itch.io/game-assets

Prefer coherent original or procedurally generated assets when mismatched packs would weaken art direction. Verify licenses and provenance. Do not rip assets from comparison games.

## Platform Gates

Combine this pack with `ios-mobile.md`, `pwa.md`, or `desktop.md` for target-specific packaging and input. Define supported hardware, resolution, frame-rate target, quality tiers, control devices, save behavior, offline/network behavior, and distributable build requirements.

Completion requires a playable integrated build, representative gameplay captures, playtest verdicts, performance profiles, and no material system or integration gap.
