# QuietRoute for iPhone

Create an iOS app that helps neurodivergent commuters plan lower-stimulation urban routes. It should combine route options with expected noise, crowding, transfers, lighting, and opportunities to pause. The product must be calm, privacy-conscious, accessible, useful offline, and excellent with Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, and one-handed use.

Research comparable navigation and accessibility apps, official Apple guidance, mapping APIs, and realistic data limitations. Use SwiftUI unless repository evidence supports a better choice.
