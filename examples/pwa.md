# Field service PWA

Build an installable PWA for small solar-installation crews to receive jobs, inspect equipment, capture photos, complete safety checklists, collect signatures, and synchronize work when connectivity returns. It must work reliably offline on iOS and Android, handle conflicts honestly, and remain usable outdoors with gloves and bright sunlight.

Research field-service products, offline synchronization patterns, camera/file APIs, and PWA platform limitations. Target a portable agent harness.
