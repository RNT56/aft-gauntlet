# Kiefern & Hufe

Build a premium German-language website for a local riding business in the Fläming region offering guided rides, riding courses, beginner experiences, children's activities, private lessons, and gift vouchers.

The site should feel cinematic, natural, trustworthy, and locally specific. It needs booking enquiries, WhatsApp contact, directions, Local SEO, accessible forms, natural layered backgrounds, and strong horse-welfare communication. Research actual competitors, current component/animation assets, and relevant equestrian media. Target Codex.
